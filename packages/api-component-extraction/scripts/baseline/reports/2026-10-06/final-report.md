## 1. Summary

**68 components across five sites, placed 238 times across their pages, for about $26.60 of model calls in
total** (both passes; Sonnet 5 for every stage).

**The $26.60 is a floor, not the spend.** It is the sum of recorded model calls, and a call killed before
it returned is never recorded. In this baseline that means the two Floor Plan Filter attempts that each ran
for 15 minutes until the Lambda killed them. These are the calls most likely to be the most expensive:
they were writing for the longest. Bounded at Sonnet 5's price for a full 15 minutes of output, the
missing spend is at most about $1.30, which would bring the total to roughly $28.

Since `c7669fc734`, a call is cut off at 5 minutes and recorded as a failed call, but its tokens still are
not: the provider reports no usage for an aborted call. Spend will keep being understated by exactly these
calls until that is closed. Every share below is given two ways:

- **Page-weighted** counts a component once per page it appears on: what a visitor of the migrated site
  sees.
- **Per component** counts each component once: how well generation works.

Page-weighted leads, because Realpage is judging a migration.

**Machine checks passed:** 55% of placements (132 of 238); 63% of components (43 of 68).

| site | platform | page-weighted | per component |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| thevibe | RealPage LeaseStar | 76% | 87% |
| parkcityplace | G5 | 64% | 50% |
| 71westland | G5 | 40% | 50% |
| 2424tulane | G5 | 40% | 45% |
| Freehill promo | G5 | 27% | 25% |

The gap between the two readings is the point. 71westland's failing components are its header, footer and
intro, which sit on many pages, so its page-weighted share is lower. Park City's failures sit on single
pages, so its share is higher.

**Other counts:**

- **Every validator green:** 63 of 68 components (93%).
- **Generated:** 67 of 68. Floor Plan Filter never finished; see below.
- **Height outside 0.67–1.5x of the source:** 13 of 66 rendered (20%).

**What 93% and 63% do not mean.** Every check we have reads content and structure — text present, props
declared, variables known, a height, an image request — and none reads appearance.

- Park City Place's Property Description passes all four validators and every machine check.
- It shows a different photograph from its section, a line pattern tiled over its body text, and the wrong
  display font.

That is not a calibration gap. The checks certify a component that is visibly wrong, so the validator set
is incomplete. It is the same shape as the composed-page signals that read healthy on pages rendering
nothing, and then on pages returning 500. Neither number measures fidelity, and neither should be quoted as
how much of a site migrated. Realpage's measure is the human verdicts.

**Floor Plan Filter (2424tulane) is a component problem, not two incidents.** It has now failed on two
independent limits: `PRESERVED_TEXT_LIMIT` (35 texts against 30) and the 15-minute Lambda limit, twice.
Its prompt contradicts itself:

- Plan's list binding put the wrong content in every list, then marks it "exactly as given":
  - tabs carry floor-plan codes and Matterport tour links;
  - filters carry layout headings;
  - floor plans read `squareFeet: "Filter By:"`, `bathrooms: "Site Map"`.
- The preserve list includes the icon names "refresh" and "add".
- The cut at 30 texts drops the third plan's prices.

Probable cause of the runaway generation (inferred): the model has no consistent answer to give. The root
defect is the list binding in sections with several sibling lists (`bindObservedValues`). The call bounds
added since make the component fail in minutes, with a reason, rather than be killed silently.

**Two things went wrong on the way and are recorded, not hidden:**

1. **Capture regression, found and fixed mid-milestone.** My stitched-screenshot fix (Part 1) scrolled the
   page, which fired the G5 sites' scroll handlers.
   - The effect: crops below the first screen were offset about 70px, sometimes with the nav bar painted over them.
   - The fix: page tiles now move the page with a transform instead (`dc0554375f`), verified on real
     pages, and the four G5 sites were re-run.
   - Pass 1 for those sites (about $9.70) is superseded and kept only as evidence.
2. **Operator intervention on thevibe.**
   - I stopped two healthy Step Functions executions on a misread of elapsed time (UTC log against local
     clock).
   - I then re-triggered Generate, which completed with all 31 components.
   - That left a pipeline claim that blocked Assemble and Promote for that run, so both were skipped.
   - Since recovered: with the claim fix deployed, Assemble was triggered on that run at 17:52 UTC and
     completed. That is the fix's first proof on live code. **Promote is held** for thevibe.
   - No output was edited.

## 2. Baseline report and review sheet

See `baseline-report.md` (per site: platform, counts, warnings, timings, every component with its height
ratio, images, validators, attempts and cost, model usage per stage, the G5 sibling comparison) and
`review-sheet.md`.

**How the review is given.** In the admin, on each component's Generate card, so the images stay behind the
admin's sign-in (imagery rights with G5 are still open). Each verdict is stored in the run's correction log:
append-only, bound to the content hash of the output it was given on, with no effect on Promote and no
carry-forward to a later run. `report.mjs` reads the verdicts back and computes the share migrated without
manual edits, page-weighted and per component.

**The verdict levels.** Two, required: **migrated without manual edits** and **needs human polish**. Reasons
are optional, from a closed list (wrong height, missing image, wrong image, background or pattern wrong,
typography wrong, text missing, arrangement wrong), plus an optional note.

- **The four-level scale in the first draft of the review sheet (accept as is, minor polish, major rework,
  reject) was ours, not Realpage's.** We set it when the review sheet was specified, and it was treated as
  Realpage's wording from then on. It is withdrawn.
- What Realpage has given us is their measure: roughly 95% migrated by the technology, the remaining 5%
  needing client approvals or human polish. The two levels follow that split. Client approval is a sign-off
  step, not a quality judgement, so it has no level.
- The labels are ours until Realpage gives us their wording, and whether they count components, sections or
  pages. A relabel changes nothing already stored: verdicts are recorded as stable keys.

**Who reviews.** The Webiny team, for this round. This is our judgement of our own output. If Realpage's
people review later, that is a separate exercise, and their verdicts are reported separately, never pooled
with ours.

**Cost:**

- The final runs cost $16.92 over 276 model calls.
- Prompt caching read 2.03M input tokens from the cache and saved $3.60 (about 24% of Generate's
  uncached cost).
- Generate averaged about $0.08–0.10 per call and 1.91 attempts per component.

**Sibling segmentation (G5):**

- Between 27% and 63% of one site's sections have a layout match on another G5 site.
- 2424tulane differs: 22 sections fall in the 800–1000px band where the container split flips, against
  0–1 on the others.
- Only the home page shares a path across sites (G5 paths carry city and state), so page-type pairing by
  path is limited.

**Measures I corrected before reporting (both in the commits):**

- **Expected images:** now counts distinct images visible in the section box, not image nodes. The old
  count flagged faithful carousels.
- **Partial image shortfalls:** informational only. A box cannot tell which stacked carousel slide is on top.

Together these moved the machine pass rate on the first four sites from 63% to 70% on the preliminary
data. Neither changed a component.

## 4. Further defects found (not fixed)

**Pipeline (the first three fixed since, on `claude/theme-app`, deployed to `dev`):**

1. **Re-triggering a running stage stranded every stage after it.** Cancel was no recovery: it marks the
   run cancelled, which the gate also refuses. **Fixed in `c5afbc3800`:**
   - the runner adopts a re-triggered task that continues a running stage;
   - the gate clears a claim whose task has finished, failed, been aborted or no longer exists, so runs
     already stuck — thevibe's included — recover by triggering the next stage.
2. **Duplicate generation.** The 8-minute component timeout was shorter than three slow attempts, so a
   component was generated twice and its result overwritten after the stage closed. **Fixed in
   `73c030a7ef`:**
   - the timeout is now 17 minutes, longer than a component task can live;
   - the first result wins, and a later task discards its own.
3. **Runaway generation calls.** No output limit and no per-call timeout, so a call could be killed with no
   result and no record. **Fixed in `c7669fc734`:**
   - output is capped at 32K tokens;
   - each call is aborted at 5 minutes, or at the time the task has left;
   - the task always records its best attempt, or why it had none.
   - An aborted call is recorded as a failed call, but its tokens are not (no usage is reported).
4. **List binding puts the wrong content in sibling lists** (`bindObservedValues`). See Floor Plan Filter
   above. It also feeds garbled "exactly as given" defaults into the prompt.
5. **Known issues still present:**
   - a file input read bare (`[object Object]`) renders blank;
   - nothing visual is validated;
   - non-content strings ("refresh", "add") are still asked for as text;
   - a fixed-position tab is cut as a section (2424tulane "Specials", 0.16x).
6. **Display fonts replaced in renders (inferred).** Seen on both components spot-checked. Needs a look at
   how theme fonts reach the sandbox.
7. **Stale task records.** A stopped Step Functions execution leaves its background task "running" forever.

**Core (for Pavel):**

8. **The background-task state machine has no timeouts.** The `Run` step invokes the Lambda with no
   `TimeoutSeconds` or `Retry`, and the execution has no overall timeout. A lost invocation would hang
   forever. That was not today's cause, but it is real.
9. **Previously reported:** remote-components has no authorization; `ComponentSandbox` accepts messages
   from any origin; the `Ai` events carry the connection.
