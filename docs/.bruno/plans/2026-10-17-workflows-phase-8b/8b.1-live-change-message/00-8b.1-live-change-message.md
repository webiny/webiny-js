Also `../00-answers.md` (Q32) and, for the inherited rules, the 8a `00-shared.md` "Global Constraints" and Rulings R202-R252 (the API-relevant ones here: R87/D132 never throw out of a notifier or handler, the storage keys stay frozen, the no-wrapper-helper rule, the DI file conventions of the folder you add to). Everything there binds every task here.

**Goal:** One tenant-scoped websocket sender, and the new silent message `workflows.reviewChanged` on top of it. The send loop of `ReviewNotifierService` moves unchanged into `ReviewWebsocketSender` (Task 1); `ReviewChangeNotifierService` and four event handlers send the message to the requester and the current owner, never to the acting user, when a review is requested and when a step starts, is approved partway or restarts (Task 2). In `packages/api-workflows`.

**Depends on:** Phase 7 (`ReviewNotifierService`, the seven notification handlers, the websocket fakes) and 8a. Nothing from 8b.2 or 8b.3. Task 2 needs Task 1. Sub-phase 8b.4 (Task 10) consumes the frame shape for its client handler, and 8b.7 mounts it.
