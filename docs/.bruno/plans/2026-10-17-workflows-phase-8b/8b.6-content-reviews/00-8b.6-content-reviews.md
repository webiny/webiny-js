**Goal:** Content Reviews gets its fifth tab, **Failed steps**, for viewers with `reassign` (or full access) only, and the list rows offer **Restart**. The tab lists AI and automation steps in `failed` on reviews in progress, oldest first, with search and the content type filter only; `?tab=failedSteps` opens it for viewers who may see it and falls back to the default tab for everyone else. A row with `flags.canRestart` shows an inline Restart button (any tab, as the server flag allows) that opens the restart dialog of Task 11 with `listRefreshed`; after the restart the row leaves the tab. In `packages/app-workflows`.

**Depends on:** 8b.4 (Task 10: `getWorkflowsPermissionSet` and `WorkflowsPermissionSet`; Task 11: the `restart` action kind through `getReviewActions`, `getListRowActions`, `ReviewActionKind` and the dialog presenter; Task 8: `restartStep` on the use cases).
