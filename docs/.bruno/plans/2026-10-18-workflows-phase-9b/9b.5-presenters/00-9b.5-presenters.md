## Review Focus for this sub-phase

Review Focus 3 (a server rule error lands on the wrong rule or cannot be cleared: Task 15 mapping by `ruleId` and by index converted at mapping time, Task 16 per-rule clearing and the live-versus-server dedupe), 4 (a saved reference renders empty or hides a restricted folder: Task 14 resolution, stale responses, retry of unresolved ids only), 5 (team data or the People step misbehaving with teams off: no lookups in Tasks 14, 14b and 16, read-only People step), 6 (Test routing shows a result that no longer matches or leaks between steps: Task 17 run token, signature at send, state per open step), 7 (Test routing prose against the server's answer: Task 14c names before composing) and 8 (the member search scoped by `teamIds`: Task 14b).
