**Size note:** Tasks 8 and 10 each stay one gate: Task 8 is all pure functions with quick tests, and Task 10's save, failures and delete share one state machine; the presenter was split into Tasks 9 and 9b along the seam of the shell and the steps.

**Goal:** The editor's logic without any UI, in `packages/app-workflows/src/editor`. First the pure core: the MobX `WorkflowDraft` (a new draft is dirty from the start, `savedOn` is the loaded string), `toInput` that emits the review defaults and keeps unknown transport ids, AI tools and the stored assignment, and the pure helpers for new steps, client validation, step type availability, summaries and warnings. Then the `WorkflowEditorPresenter` in two tasks: the shell (permission gate, adapter init, options with a status per list, the paged and searched model list, the bound workflow, team names, the leave prompt, the render-ready VM) and the steps (create, the step cards, the type picker, add, move and delete, the open step, the accessors the step drawer reads, the team search). Then save (client validation, the store call, every failure mapped onto the step, field, banner or dialog) and delete (including the blocked case), each clearing the review cache.

**Depends on:** 9a.3 (Task 5, 5b, 5c domain types, error codes, use-case interfaces, toasts and fixtures; Task 6 `invalidateWorkflows` and the `createDeferred` test helper), 9a.2 Task 3 (`JsonSchema`, `createJsonSchemaValidator`, used by Task 8's validation and warnings). Tasks 11-14 (forms and drawer) and 15-19 (view) consume it; Task 14 reads the draft and the option lists through the presenter's accessors.
