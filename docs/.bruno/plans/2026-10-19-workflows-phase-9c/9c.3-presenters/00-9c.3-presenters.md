## Review Focus for this sub-phase

- **Review Focus 1** (a save drops or changes entries): Task 7 (`canAdd` false and `openAdd` / `openEdit` / `openRemove` no-ops while loading, after a failed load and with no access), Task 9 (payload = the source's list plus the one entry, appended on add, replaced in place on edit; the returned record goes to `applySaved`), Task 10 (payload = the list without the entry; others unchanged and in order).
- **Review Focus 2** (wrong day or instant): Task 9 (unchanged date keeps the stored string, also a non-23:59 instant; changed date recomputed for the zone, New York DST day; `Pick today or a later date.` only when changed; `minDate` is local midnight of today in the viewer's zone).
- **Review Focus 3** (status and until label): Task 7 (rows per tab with fake timers at `endsOn - 1` and `endsOn`; labels per zone).
- **Review Focus 4** (names): Task 8 (chunks of 200, `missingIds` to `Deleted user`, failure banner with ids, retry asks only unresolved ids, stale run dropped after a reload and a newer save, `NotAuthorized` to no access, a picked user is never looked up), Tasks 9 and 10 (a deleted user's entry saves and removes).
- **Review Focus 6** (gate): Task 7 (`canManage: false` makes no call and shows no access; `NotAuthorized` load to no access), Tasks 7, 9, 10 (`app.test.tsx` flag on and off).
- **Review Focus 7** (dialog failure): Tasks 9 and 10 (transport and server alert texts, values and row kept, editing a field keeps the alert, the next submit clears it, double submit and cancel while saving ignored).
- **Review Focus 10** (reason): Task 9 (payload trimmed, whitespace-only `null`, 500 cut through `normaliseReason`).
- Render-ready VMs (R234): no domain object, callback or presenter in any VM; `minDate` is a `Date` built by the presenter (R337); the dialogs reach the page only through `ExclusionsSource` passed in `init`.
- No wrapper helpers: every `getSettings`, `updateSettings`, `listUsers` and `toasts.success` call is inline in the presenter method that needs it; mutations after an `await` go through named private methods.
